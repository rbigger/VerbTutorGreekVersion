How Adaptive Spaced Repetition Works

1. Learn with feedback-driven intervals

After answering each question, you'll rate how well you knew the answer using four difficulty levels:

  • Hard - You struggled with this verb (review again in 10 minutes)
  • Medium - You knew it but had to think (review again in 1 hour)
  • Easy - You knew it quickly (review again in 1 day)
  • Learned - You've mastered it (review again in 5 days)
2. Customize your learning pace

You can adjust the intervals for each difficulty level in the Settings tab to match your learning style.

3. Focus on what needs practice

The system prioritizes verbs that are due for review, with harder items appearing more frequently.

Why this works

Research shows that successful retrieval strengthens memory, and the optimal time to review is just before you would naturally forget. This system helps you practice at the right intervals for maximum retention.
